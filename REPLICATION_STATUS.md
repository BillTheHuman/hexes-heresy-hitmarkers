# Replication status

**Status: first-party, AI-assisted public release; no independent
replication.**

“First-party” means that the participant and the AI-assisted project
environment created the private implementation, operated or inspected the
tests, summarized the evidence, and prepared this release. No outside person
or laboratory has reproduced the implementation.

## Current status by level

| Level | Status | What the status means |

## What the public verifier does and does not establish

A successful verification run establishes that the exact public files match
their manifest and satisfy the encoded format and relationship rules. It makes
alteration, omission, and certain categories of overstatement detectable.

It does not:

- authenticate that a private event occurred;
- prove that a sanitized transcription matches a withheld source;
- validate the participant's ownership or acquisition account;
- recreate the private build or execution;
- establish audiovisual or gameplay fidelity;
- turn participant testimony into independent observation; or
- provide legal clearance.

## How future replication should be recorded

A future independent effort should state the investigator, environment,
versioned protocol, input authority, artifact-binding method, expected and
observed results, failures, retained evidence, and conflicts of interest. It
should be added as a new evidence event rather than used to relabel the present
first-party record.

The phrase “independently replicated” should appear in project publicity only
after an actually independent party has performed and documented the relevant
work. Merely cloning this report, running its verifier, or reproducing its JSON
classification is public record verification, not execution replication.
